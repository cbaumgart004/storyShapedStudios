She asked directly in the notes doc: "(If you read this and have any notes on
grammar or redundancy please let me know)". Notes are written; they need sending
to her and her decisions taking. Nothing here changes code until she rules.

Findings on the 2026-08-03 copy:

- **Three competing world-superlatives.** "the world's most trusted source"
  (Education Through Research), "the world's leading authority" (Our Story), and
  "the largest retailer of uranium glass beads in the world" (A Space for
  Makers). The first two are near-synonyms; the third is concrete and
  verifiable. Suggest keeping the third and one of the first two.
- **One real grammatical error.** "What began as a personal fascination grew
  into the world's leading authority on uranium glass jewelry and beads" — a
  fascination cannot become an authority. Needs a subject swap.
- **Broken parallel.** "Thousands of hours of research, an international
  community of collectors, and a passion for preserving these remarkable
  artifacts eventually led her to teach herself jewelry making" — items 1 and 3
  are things she brought, item 2 is a group of people, and does not read as a
  cause of her learning to make jewelry.
- **Repeated openings.** "Every piece" opens three of the seven values.
  "Whether" opens two values plus Our Jewelry. "Remarkable" appears four times
  across the page.
- **Overlapping values.** Preserving History, Restoration & Renewal and
  Honoring the Material all land on carrying pieces across generations, and Our
  Story's closing sentence says it a fourth time. Seven values is also a lot of
  stacked text for a splash page; four would hit harder, and the overlapping
  three are the cut candidates.
- **Small fixes.** "highest quality Czech glass" wants a hyphen; "with over 200
  unique bead designs both vintage and newly made" wants a comma before "both".
- **Tone.** Education Through Research is the only value hedged as an aspiration
  ("we strive to be") while the other six are declarative, so it reads weaker.
