What each channel takes from one sale · US seller, US buyer · rates as of November 2026

Before pricing on them, confirm on Etsy's, eBay's and Stripe's own fee pages. eBay's jewelry rate is the one guides disagree on: a flat 15%, or 15% up to $5,000 and lower above it. That makes no difference at our prices.

One sale, three ways

Example figures: a $45 piece with $5 shipping. Change any number above. "You keep" is what reaches you after fees and before your making cost; "profit" subtracts the making cost too. Sales tax is collected and paid by Etsy and eBay, and by you on the site; it is not counted as yours.

What this means for us

  • Per sale, on the example above the site through Stripe costs the least (about 3.7%), Etsy next (about 10.6%), eBay the most (about 17%). An Etsy sale through an Offsite Ad costs more than eBay.
  • Etsy and eBay charge for finding the buyer. The site's lower fee only pays off on sales the site brings in itself.
  • eBay's 15% includes the sales tax, so its effective cut of what you keep is higher than 15%.
  • At low prices the flat fees ($0.25, $0.30, $0.40) matter most: on a $10 piece they add 3 to 4 percentage points.
